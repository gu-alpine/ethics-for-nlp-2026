## Registration and venue

To attend the workshop either please register for the [SLTC 2026](https://sltc.confetti.events/) conference.

Further information about the conference and the venue is available [here](https://www.ltu.se/en/research/research-subjects/machine-learning/the-swedish-language-technology-conference).

The workshop will take place in room A3101a.


## Information for presenters

* Keynote talks will be 45 minutes.
  * 10 minutes of these are intended for questions.
* Short talks will be 15 minutes each.
  * 5 minutes of these are intended for questions.
* Participants will be invited to Further discussions on the topics presented in the concluding session of the workshop.

To enable quicker speaker switching, please upload your presentations slides before the talk as  a PDP, PowerPoint or Keynote format to [this shared folder](https://gunet-my.sharepoint.com/:f:/g/personal/simon_dobnik_gu_se/IgBvvyLwb12hTam9i8nmepJeAVAcwP5uViIqb5WIBvvoRns).
* Name your files as ``lastname_title.pdf`` to make it easier for us to know which presentation belongs to who.
* The folder will be accessible from the presentation computer in the room.
* If you would like to update your slides, simply upload a new version with the same filename.

If you have have any additional presentation requirements, e.g. slides in different format, playing video and sound, etc., please [contact Simon](https://www.gu.se/en/about/find-staff/simondobnik) in advance.
